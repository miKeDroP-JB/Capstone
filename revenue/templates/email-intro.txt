## BEST PRACTICES

**Subject Lines That Work:**
- "Add 20% more appointments to [COMPANY]"
- "Quick question about your HVAC leads"
- "How [COMPETITOR] books 25% more"
- "Re: Your after-hours calls"

**First Sentence Rules:**
- Lead with value, not who you are
- Ask a question they'll say "yes" to
- Reference their specific problem
- Keep it under 15 words

**Body Copy:**
- 3-5 sentences max
- One clear benefit
- Social proof if possible
- Zero risk statement
- Clear call to action

**Call to Action:**
- Always give 2 specific time options
- Never "let me know when you're free"
- Make it easy to say yes
- Include your phone number

**Send Timing:**
- Best: Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10am
- Good: Monday 10am-12pm
- Avoid: Friday PM, weekends, before 7am, after 6pm

**Follow-Up Cadence:**
If no response:
- Day 3: Send Template 2 (different angle)
- Day 7: Send Template 3 (social proof)
- Day 14: Final follow-up: "Should I close your file or...?"

**Don't chase forever. 3 touches is enough.**
