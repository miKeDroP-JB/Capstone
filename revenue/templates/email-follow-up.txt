## FOLLOW-UP BEST PRACTICES

**Timing:**
- Day 1: Within 1 hour (while it's fresh)
- Day 2: Morning (before they get busy)
- Day 4: Mid-day (Tuesday-Thursday best)
- Day 7: Morning (final touch)

**Tone:**
- Helpful, not pushy
- Professional, not desperate
- Brief, not verbose
- Personal, not templated

**Include:**
- Clear next step
- Easy way to respond
- Your phone number (always)
- Value reminder

**Don't:**
- Send more than 3-4 follow-ups
- Sound desperate
- Guilt-trip them
- Discount too aggressively (devalues service)

**Do:**
- Make it easy to say yes
- Address specific concerns
- Provide social proof
- Give them an out ("should I close your file?")
