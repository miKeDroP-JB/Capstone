# HVAC SALES AGENT - PHONE SCRIPT
# ================================
# Performance-Based AI Sales Agent
# Read this 3x before making calls. Then customize to your style.

## OPENING (First 15 seconds - CRITICAL)

"Hi, this is [YOUR NAME] calling for [THEIR NAME/OWNER].
Is this a good time for a quick call?"

[IF NO:]
"No problem. When's a better time? I'll call back then."
[Schedule callback, hang up]

[IF YES:]
"Perfect. This'll take 2 minutes max.

I help HVAC companies book 20-30% more appointments using AI,
with zero upfront cost. You only pay if it actually works.

Sound interesting?"

[WAIT FOR RESPONSE]

---

## IF THEY SAY "MAYBE" OR "TELL ME MORE"

"Here's how it works:

We've built an AI sales agent that calls your leads automatically
and books appointments directly into your calendar.

The unique part - it's 100% performance-based. You only pay us 10%
of the ADDITIONAL profit it generates. So if we book an appointment
that turns into a $5,000 job, you pay us $500. If we book nothing,
you pay nothing.

Most HVAC companies lose 30-40% of potential revenue from slow
follow-up. People call, leave voicemail, and by the time you
call back, they've hired someone else. This fixes that problem.

Does that make sense?"

[WAIT FOR RESPONSE]

---

## HANDLING "HOW DOES IT WORK?"

"Great question. Here's the process:

1. We integrate with your current system - takes about 15 minutes
2. When leads come in, our AI calls them within 60 seconds
3. It follows your script, answers questions, books appointments
4. You get a text: 'Appointment booked for Tuesday at 2pm with John Smith'
5. You show up, do the work, get paid
6. We track conversions and bill you 10% monthly

The AI sounds natural - I can play you a sample call if you want."

[IF THEY WANT TO HEAR SAMPLE:]
"I'll send you a link to listen. Better yet, how about a quick
15-minute demo? I can show you the dashboard, play sample calls,
and we can even test it with one of your real leads.

I have time tomorrow morning at 10am or Thursday afternoon at 2pm.
Which works better?"

---

## HANDLING "WE ALREADY HAVE SOMEONE"

"That's great! Are they booking 100% of your leads? Because most
companies are only booking 60-70%, which means 30-40% are falling
through the cracks.

Our system doesn't replace your current process - it supplements it.
Think of it as backup. When your team is busy, the AI picks up the
slack. You still keep your current setup.

And remember - no upfront cost. If it doesn't book appointments,
it costs you nothing. What do you have to lose?"

---

## HANDLING "TOO EXPENSIVE"

"I totally get it. That's exactly why we do performance-based pricing.

You don't pay anything upfront. Not $500, not $100, not even $1.

You ONLY pay 10% AFTER we've already made you money. So if we
generate an extra $10,000 in revenue for you, you pay us $1,000.
But you've still netted $9,000 you wouldn't have had otherwise.

It's literally impossible to lose money on this. Make sense?"

---

## HANDLING "I NEED TO THINK ABOUT IT"

"Totally fair. Let me ask - what specifically would you need to see
or know to feel comfortable moving forward?

[LISTEN TO THEIR ANSWER]

[IF IT'S SOMETHING YOU CAN ADDRESS:]
"Okay, I can help with that. How about this - let me send you some
information about [their concern], and I'll call you back on
[DAY] at [TIME]. Does that work?"

[IF IT'S JUST STALLING:]
"I understand. Here's what I'd recommend: Let's do a 15-minute demo.
No commitment, just show you how it works. If you like it, great.
If not, no hard feelings. Fair enough?"

---

## HANDLING "SEND ME INFORMATION"

"Absolutely. What's the best email?

[GET EMAIL]

Great, I'll send that over in the next hour. But real quick - since
I have you on the phone, let me ask: if the information looks good,
what's your typical next step? Would you want to see a demo, or...?"

[GOAL: Try to book demo NOW rather than email → wait → maybe call back]

---

## CLOSING FOR THE DEMO

"So here's what I'm thinking: Let's do a quick 15-minute demo.
I'll show you the dashboard, play you some sample calls, and we
can even test it with one of your actual leads if you want.

I have two slots available:
- Tomorrow (Tuesday) at 10:00am
- Thursday at 2:00pm

Which one works better for your schedule?"

[USE "THIS OR THAT" - NOT "DO YOU WANT TO?"]

---

## CONFIRMING THE DEMO

"Perfect. So I've got you down for [DAY] at [TIME].

What's the best number to call you on?
[CONFIRM NUMBER]

And just in case, what's your email so I can send a calendar invite?
[GET EMAIL]

Great! You'll get a calendar invite with a Zoom link in the next
few minutes. See you [DAY] at [TIME]!"

---

## IF THEY SAY "NOT INTERESTED"

"No problem, I totally understand. Just out of curiosity - is it
the timing, or is it something about the service itself?"

[IF TIMING:]
"Got it. When would be a better time to follow up? 3 months? 6 months?"

[IF SERVICE:]
"Fair enough. Is it okay if I check back in a few months just in
case anything changes?"

[IF HARD NO:]
"I totally get it. Thanks for your time, and good luck with the
business!"

[Hang up professionally, move to next call]

---

## VOICEMAIL SCRIPT

"Hi [NAME], this is [YOUR NAME] calling from Elite HVAC Solutions.

I help HVAC companies book more appointments using AI with zero
upfront cost - you only pay if it actually generates revenue.

If you're interested in hearing how we can help you capture
30-40% more appointments, give me a call back at [YOUR NUMBER].

Again, that's [YOUR NAME] at [YOUR NUMBER]. Thanks!"

[Speak clearly, not too fast, don't sound like spam]

---

## KEY PRINCIPLES

1. **CONFIDENCE** - You're offering something valuable. Act like it.

2. **BREVITY** - Keep it short. Don't ramble. Make your point and ask for demo.

3. **VALUE FOCUS** - Always focus on THEIR benefit (more appointments, no risk)

4. **SCARCITY** - Limited demo slots creates urgency

5. **PERFORMANCE-BASED** - Hammer this home. No risk for them.

6. **LISTENING** - Listen for pain points. When they mention "hard to reach people"
   or "leads going cold" - that's your opening.
