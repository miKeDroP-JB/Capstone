## DEMO FLOW (15-20 minutes)

### PART 1: INTRO & AGENDA (2 minutes)

"Hey [NAME], thanks for jumping on! Can you hear me okay?

[CONFIRM AUDIO/VIDEO]

Perfect. So we've got about 15-20 minutes. Here's what I'll show you:

1. Quick overview of how the system works
2. Sample AI call (you'll hear how natural it sounds)
3. The dashboard (where you track everything)
4. Pricing breakdown
5. Q&A and next steps

Sound good?

[WAIT FOR YES]

Great! Before we dive in, tell me quick - what made you interested
in taking this call? What's the biggest challenge you're facing
with lead follow-up right now?"

[LISTEN CAREFULLY - Their answer is your demo focus]

---

### PART 2: HOW IT WORKS (3 minutes)

"Okay, so here's how the whole system works:

[SHARE SCREEN - Show simple diagram or flowchart]

1. **Lead comes in** - Could be from Google, Facebook, Yelp, wherever
2. **AI gets notified** - Within 60 seconds, our system sees the lead
3. **AI makes the call** - Calls the prospect using a natural voice
4. **Conversation happens** - AI follows your script, answers questions
5. **Appointment booked** - If interested, books directly into your calendar
6. **You get notified** - Text message: 'Appointment booked with John Smith Tuesday 2pm'
7. **You show up** - Do the work, close the deal, get paid
8. **We track it** - System tracks which appointments came from AI
9. **Monthly billing** - You pay 10% of converted jobs

The beauty is - you're not replacing your current process. This runs
alongside it. When you're busy, the AI handles the overflow. When
you're slow, it's filling your calendar.

Make sense so far?

[ANSWER QUESTIONS]

---

"Okay, now the fun part. Let me play you a sample call so you can

[PLAY SAMPLE CALL - 1-2 minutes]

Now, that was our default script. But here's the thing - we customize

Some clients want it super professional. Others want it more casual.
Whatever matches your brand, we match it.

How would you want yours to sound?"

[DISCUSS THEIR PREFERENCES]

### PART 5: PRICING BREAKDOWN (3 minutes)

"Okay, let's talk numbers. Because this is where we're different
from everyone else.

[SHARE SCREEN - Show pricing sheet]

**Setup:**
- One-time setup: $200 (or $0 if you prefer pure performance)
- Gets you configured, script written, voice trained
- Takes about 1 hour, done remotely

**Monthly:**
- You pay 10% of ONLY the jobs that came from AI-booked appointments
- We track everything automatically
- Monthly invoice, net-30 terms

**Example Month:**
- AI books 20 appointments
- 10 convert to jobs (50% close rate - typical)
- Average job: $3,000
- Total revenue from AI: $30,000
- Your profit: $30,000 - costs - $3,000 (10% to us) = ~$24,000 net
- You pay us: $3,000

**But here's the key:**
That $30,000 in revenue? You WEREN'T getting it before. It's pure
incremental revenue. Leads that were falling through the cracks.

And if we book zero appointments that convert? You pay zero.

Does that math make sense?

[ANSWER QUESTIONS]"

## HANDLING OBJECTIONS DURING DEMO

### "I need to think about it"

"Totally fair. Let me ask - what specifically are you unsure about?
Is it the pricing, the technology, the time commitment, or...?"

[IDENTIFY SPECIFIC CONCERN, ADDRESS IT]

"How about this - let's schedule a follow-up for [2-3 DAYS]. That
gives you time to think, and if you have any questions between now
and then, just shoot me an email. Fair?"

### "I need to talk to my partner/wife/business partner"

"Makes total sense. Big decision, you should discuss it.

Here's what might help: I can send you a quick one-pager that
summarizes everything we discussed. You can share that with [PARTNER],
and then how about the three of us jump on a quick call [DAY]?

Or if it's easier, you chat with them first and let me know when
you're ready. What works better?"

### "How do I know it will work?"

"Great question. Honest answer - I can't guarantee results because
I don't control your close rate or your pricing.

What I CAN tell you is:
1. The AI will book appointments - that part works 100%
2. We've seen 10-30% increase in booked appointments across our clients
3. Your worst case is you pay $200 setup and it doesn't work - we refund it
4. Your best case is you're booking 20-30% more jobs

The only way to know for YOUR business is to try it. That's why we
do performance-based pricing - removes the risk.

Want to give it a shot?"

### "That's too expensive"

"I hear you. Let's break it down differently:

If the AI books just ONE extra job per month that you wouldn't have
gotten otherwise, how much is that worth?

[THEY SAY $2,000-5,000]

Right. And you'd be paying us 10% of that, so $200-500.

But you're netting $1,800-4,500 you wouldn't have had.

And that's if it books ONE job. Most clients see 5-15 additional
jobs per month.

So the question isn't 'is it expensive' - the question is 'is it
profitable?' And the math clearly says yes.

Make sense?"
