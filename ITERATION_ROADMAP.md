## 🚀 FEATURE FLAGGING

### How We Ship New Features:

**Stage 1: Internal Testing (1 week)**
- Deploy to our own systems
- Test with synthetic data
- Fix obvious bugs

**Stage 2: Beta Customers (2 weeks)**
- Deploy to 5-10 willing beta customers
- Feature flag ON for them
- Feature flag OFF for everyone else
- Gather feedback, iterate

**Stage 3: Gradual Rollout (1 month)**
- Week 1: 10% of customers
- Week 2: 25% of customers
- Week 3: 50% of customers
- Week 4: 100% of customers

**Stage 4: Default ON**
- Feature flag removed
- Now part of core system
- Documented and supported

**This Allows:**
- Fast iteration (ship weekly)
- Low risk (gradual rollout)
- Easy rollback (flip flag OFF)
- Customer feedback (beta group)
