### Day 2: Understand Love Scores

**The Formula:**
```
Love Score = (Value Created + Regeneration) / (Extraction + 1)
```

**In English:**
- Value Created = How many people you help × How much you help them
- Regeneration = How much you give back (community share, free tier, open source)
- Extraction = How much you take (revenue, costs)

**The Threshold:**
- 95-100% = Exceptional Love 🌟 (pure giving)
- 85-94% = High Love 💚 (strong net positive)
- 70-84% = Good Love 💛 (net positive, APPROVED)
- 50-69% = Neutral 💙 (balanced)
- <50% = Low Love 🧡 (more taking than giving, needs improvement)

**Goal:** Always aim for 70%+ (net positive)

**Examples:**

**98% Love - Elder Care AI**
```

      + Reduced suffering
```
